MODEL NOTE / v1.0

Show your working.

Implied token market capitalization is a scenario, not a forecast or an airdrop valuation. Principal and fees are not expected rewards.

TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × history factor

How the calculation works

  1. Use traded-token protocols with a reported positive market cap and TVL. Exclude wrapped / staked products, bridges and CEXs from comparables.
  2. Winsorize each market-cap / TVL ratio to 0.05–5×. Blend the category median with the universe median using n / (n + 8). Sparse groups shrink toward the universe.
  3. Growth factor = (1 + clipped 7-day growth / 100)^0.30, with growth clipped to −50%…100%. Missing growth uses 0%, flagged in detail.
  4. History factor = 0.70 + 0.30 × min(tracking age, 1,095) / 1,095. Unknown tracking age uses 365 days. Tracking age is not protocol launch age.
  5. The low/high band is an assumption sensitivity envelope: ±35%, widened by 15 points for fewer than four peers and 10 points each for missing age/growth. It is not a statistical confidence interval.

Boundaries

TVL is not revenue, tokenholder cash flow or safety. Category peers may not be economically comparable. A protocol may already have a token absent from this record. Plans are conditional product workflows, not verified eligibility. Fees, access, rates and withdrawal delays require independent checks. No protocol or chain receives a special score by name.
